About me

Ecology × DNA × computation.

I am interested in the point where ecological questions, molecular data and computational reproducibility meet.

My research development has moved from biotechnology and molecular biology into molecular biodiversity and mitochondrial genomics, and then into eDNA metabarcoding, forest ecology, Pool-seq and landscape genomics. The common thread is using molecular information to understand biological variation in real ecological systems.

I also build workflows when a research problem needs a transparent computational route. PoLoCo, PoMito and IxMeta reflect that part of my work: analytical decisions should be inspectable, reproducible and transferable rather than hidden in one-off scripts.
